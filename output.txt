INT. PADDY'S PUB - DAY
The bar is empty of customers. DENNIS is holding a small, pristine dog carrier. MAC is standing beside him, wearing tight athletic gear. DEE is behind the bar, looking annoyed.
DENNIS
I’m just saying, Mac, the implication is that if I bring the dog, women will trust me more. It’s a classic "soft guy" move.
MAC
That dog is not a "soft" dog, Dennis. That dog has tiny, frantic energy. It makes you look frantic.
DENNIS
(Getting angry)
I am not frantic! I am calm! I am a five-star man with a golden retriever energy, even if this is a Pomeranian!
DEE
(Shouting from the bar)
You’re both idiots. No one is coming into this bar, with or without a dog, because it smells like cleaning chemicals and trash.
MAC
It smells like victory, Dee!
CHARLIE bursts through the front door, wearing a yellow hazmat suit, carrying a bucket of green liquid.
CHARLIE
Guys! Guys! I did it. I finally perfected the Paddy’s Pub green paint-beer. It’s 100% house paint, but I mixed it with a little bit of Fight Milk so it’s "nutritious."
DENNIS
(Disgusted)
Charlie, you cannot serve paint to people!
CHARLIE
It’s not paint! It’s a concoction! It’s for the Health Inspector. I’m going to drink this, tell him it’s delicious, and then he’s gonna throw up, and then we win!
DEE
You’re going to die, Charlie.
CHARLIE
(Ignoring her)
Wait, where’s Frank? Frank! I need you to hold my hair back!
FRANK rises from behind the bar, holding a gun.
FRANK
Stop talking about the paint! I just bought a guy’s car for $500, and I think he’s following me to get it back. I’m gonna blast him!
Frank runs toward the door.
DENNIS
Frank, don't blast anyone!
Dennis tries to run after Frank, but drops the dog carrier. The Pomeranian is not inside; it is just a picture of a Pomeranian.
MAC
(Looking at the picture)
You didn't even bring a real dog?
DENNIS
(Screaming)
THE IMPLICATION, MAC! THE IMPLICATION!
Dee hits her head on the beer tap. Charlie drinks from the green bucket and immediately collapses.
[SCENE END]
